The problem

3D room capture still assumes one person, one camera, and a lot of time.

01

One path leaves holes.

A single person misses angles, corners, and the backs of objects.

02

People become geometry.

Moving bodies turn into floating noise unless they are removed first.

03

Good results take setup.

Many workflows still need specialist hardware or long processing.

How to get useful coverage

  1. Hold the phone sideways.
  2. Walk a slow sideways arc—do not just spin.
  3. Keep a shared landmark in view.
  4. Finish on that landmark, then submit.
